01 / RESEARCH

Methods for the strong-field universe.

My work connects exact black-hole perturbation theory with efficient computation for gravitational-wave astronomy.

01

Gravitational-wave fluxes

A unified confluent-Heun framework for generic Kerr orbits combines angular and radial Teukolsky solutions with stable source integration. In a published 168-mode benchmark, relative errors are of order 10−11 in double precision, with typical 3–13× speedups against comparison software.

Read the 2026 paper ↗
02

Black-hole quasinormal modes

Analytic continuation enables computation of modes crossing or lying on the negative imaginary axis. Schwarzschild and Kerr spectra were checked with scattering amplitudes, including overtones up to n = 41 and angular indices up to ℓ = 16.

Read the 2025 paper ↗
03

Scattering & total-transmission modes

Recent work traces globally continuous Kerr total-transmission-mode families and explores exceptional-point coalescence. Earlier work extended exact radiation calculations to gravitational, electromagnetic and scalar perturbations.

Read the preprint ↗
